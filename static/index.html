Understanding Ex-Dividend Cutoff Dates for UK & Global Shares

The Ex-Dividend Date is the critical date by which you must own the share. You MUST buy shares on or before the last trading day prior to the ex-dividend date to qualify for the dividend payout. If you purchase on or after the ex-dividend date, the seller keeps the dividend!

Stock Picker Tabs & Methodology Guide

A comprehensive breakdown of how each screening tab operates, how algorithms rank market movers, what every metric means, and how to use them across the UK (FTSE), US (NASDAQ / Dow Jones), and Indian (NIFTY) markets.

Tab 1

Trending Stocks

Core Objective: Spot early-stage breakouts, aggressive momentum surges, and institutional accumulation before the wider market catches on.

🧠 Multi-Factor Trending Algorithm (Score 0 - 100):
  • Relative Volume Surge (up to 35 pts): Volume $> 1.5\times$ to $3.0\times$ normal 10-day averages signals heavyweight institutional buyers ("Smart Money").
  • Price Velocity (up to 35 pts): Aggressive upward momentum over 1-day and 1-week periods.
  • 52-Week High Proximity (up to 15 pts): Trading within 3% of 52-week highs indicates breakout strength with zero overhead resistance.
  • Moving Average Alignment (up to 15 pts): Bullish trend confirmation above both 50 DMA and 200 DMA.
🏷️ Badge Legend:
🔥 Vol Surge 2.5x 🚀 52W High Breakout ⚡ +6.4% 1W Run ✨ Bullish Trend
💡 Best for: Growth investors, momentum swing traders, and breakout runners.
Tab 2

Value Gainers (% Increase)

Core Objective: Screen top performing shares that have gained value over your choice of 1 Day, 1 Week, or 1 Month, with full user customization.

⏱️ Timeframe Analysis:
  • 1 Day (1D): Pinpoints immediate market catalysts, earnings surprises, or broker upgrades today.
  • 1 Week (1W): 5-day cumulative return. Filters out 1-day noise to identify weekly leadership.
  • 1 Month (1M): ~21-day intermediate swing. Reveals sustainable macro and sector rotation trends.
🎛️ Customizable % Gain Threshold:

Use the quick preset pills (`>0%`, `+2%`, `+5%`, `+10%`) or drag the interactive slider and type an exact custom percentage (e.g. `+3.5%` or `+7.2%`) to filter only shares matching your target criteria.

💡 Pro Tip: Look for shares showing consistent positive gains across both 1W and 1M with high trading volume.
Tab 3

Value Decliners (Dip Finder)

Core Objective: Identify oversold pullbacks, market corrections, and temporary discounts for contrarian value investing.

📉 Contrarian Value Investing:

Market sell-offs frequently punish high-quality companies indiscriminately due to broad index volatility. The Dip Finder highlights stocks experiencing short-term pullbacks ($\le -2\%$, $\le -5\%$, or custom e.g. $-4.0\%$).

🛡️ Quality & Margin of Safety Checks:
  • Distance to 52-Week Low: Stocks trading within 5% of their 52-week low often find strong institutional support.
  • Valuation Multiples: Check the P/E ratio and dividend yield — a falling price pushes dividend yield higher, creating a cash-flow buffer.
💡 Quote: "Whether we're talking about socks or stocks, I like buying quality merchandise when it is marked down." — Warren Buffett
Tab 4

Highest Trading Volume & Liquidity

Core Objective: Track market liquidity and follow where the largest institutional capital flows are taking place.

💰 The 3 Volume Dimensions:
  • 1. Shares Traded (Volume): Raw count of shares exchanged over 1D, 1W, or 1M.
  • 2. Cash Turnover (£ / $ / ₹): Calculated as $\text{Volume} \times \text{Price}$. This is the true institutional footprint. A 5M share trade in £36 Shell is £180M of capital, far more impactful than 10M shares in a 50p penny stock.
  • 3. Relative Volume (RVOL): The ratio of current volume to the 10-day average. $\text{RVOL} \ge 2.0\times$ means twice the normal volume is flooding in.
🔍 Why Volume Precedes Price:

Heavy volume on up days indicates institutional accumulation; heavy volume on down days flags institutional distribution.

💡 Best for: Liquidity screening, identifying high-conviction institutional block orders, and avoid illiquid traps.
Tab 5

Upcoming Dividends & Ex-Div Cutoffs

Core Objective: Track scheduled corporate dividend payouts, calculate cash income, and never miss the mandatory purchase deadline.

⚠️ The Golden Rule of Ex-Dividend Dates:

To receive a dividend, you MUST own the shares BEFORE the Ex-Dividend Date (by prior market close). If you buy on or after the Ex-Dividend date, the shares trade "ex" (without) dividend, and the previous shareholder receives the payout.

📅 Dividend Timeline Steps:
1. Declaration
2. Ex-Div Cutoff
3. Record Date
4. Pay Date (£)
